The approved 1800 × 1840 artwork is included in the Truth carousel as
`public/truth/nolan-time-dark.svg` and `nolan-time-light.svg`. The revision removes
all explanatory prose, years, legends and in-diagram annotations. Each film has

Original research notes for Saneel's approved artwork follow.

## Visual contract

An interpretive atlas of Nolan's **13 directed feature films**, from Following
through The Odyssey. It does not cover shorts, producer-only credits, or attempt
to represent every scene or every temporal device in each film.

The diagrams separate narrative editing, subjective experience and physical
time within the films. The last category describes the films' worlds: Tenet's
inversion is fictional, not an established physical effect. Interstellar uses
relativity, with an extreme fictional setting.

All curves, mesh densities, spatial distances, fragment counts and arrow
positions are authored illustrative geometry. They are **not measured durations,
scene counts, exact editing orders or physical simulations**. The film-specific
numeric labels are the eight-year interval before The Dark Knight Rises; the
hour/seven-year relationship in Interstellar; Dunkirk's week/day/hour strands;
and Oppenheimer's 1954/1959 framing hearings. Release years use first release,
including festival premieres; Following is often listed as 1999 for its wider
release, but first premiered in 1998.

Inception's diagram isolates waking life and the three coordinated dream levels
of the main heist, not limbo or every dream visited in the film. The Odyssey is
shown as an interpretation of homecoming and embedded recollection, not an exact
map of its editing order. The closing sentence is original interpretation.

## Files and inspection

- `nolan-time.mjs`: editable deterministic SVG generator; no external libraries.
- `nolan-time-dark.svg` / `nolan-time-light.svg`: resolution-independent artwork.
- `nolan-time-dark.png` / `nolan-time-light.png`: 1800 × 2290 raster exports.

Both themes were rendered and inspected at full resolution and half-width.
Text extents were checked for canvas clipping; the Inception labels and kick
connector were adjusted after inspection. No film stills or third-party artwork
are copied. The diagrams and copy are original, source-grounded interpretations.

Suggested alt: “Thirteen schematic diagrams compare how Christopher Nolan uses
time: fragmented chronology, converging forward and reverse sequences, constant
daylight, memories, nested diaries and dreams, shared deadlines, unequal clocks,
inversion, historical testimony, and homecoming.”
