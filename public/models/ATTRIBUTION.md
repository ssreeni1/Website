# Formula car model attribution

`formula1.glb` is an optimized and visually restyled derivative of
“McLaren MP4/5 — Formula 1” by dark_igorek, downloaded from
https://www.get3dmodels.com/vehicles/formula-1-car/

The source model is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Geometry was simplified, textures were compressed, and materials are replaced
at runtime for this website’s technical wireframe presentation.
